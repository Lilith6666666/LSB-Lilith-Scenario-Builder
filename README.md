# LSB: Lilith Scenario Builder

*Hi, I'm Lilith! You bring the story idea; I'll help build the world around it. ☕*

**Lilith Scenario Builder (LSB)** is a community-made toolkit for creating and improving **AI Dungeon** scenarios. Less time formatting, more time imagining what happens next.

### What it does

- Creates complete scenarios with an opening, Plot Essentials, AI Instructions, Author's Note, and Story Cards.
- Produces a copy-ready **scenario `.txt`** and a matching, importable **Story Cards `.json`**.
- Helps you **revise, expand, merge, audit, and validate** existing scenarios.
- Prioritizes player freedom, consistent characters, and efficient context. No scripts required.

### Get started

Choose what works for your AI assistant:

- **[Instructions (.txt)](LILITH_SCENARIO_INSTRUCTIONS.txt)** — the complete specification, usable as reference material.
- **[Skill package (.zip)](LILITH_SCENARIO_BUILDER_v1.0.zip)** — the reusable skill, examples, and validation utilities for skill-compatible setups.

Then try a prompt like:

> Create a cozy mystery in a rainy seaside town using Lilith Scenario Builder. Give me the complete scenario TXT and Story Cards JSON.

**A little heads-up:** Importing Story Cards JSON into AI Dungeon **replaces your existing cards**. Export a backup or merge your collections first!
